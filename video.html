Videography



This music video was filmed collaboratively as a group project, with all members contributing to the production process. While the footage was created together, each member edited their own version independently, interpreting the material through their personal creative perspective.

Since I was unfamiliar with the song initially, I began by studying the lyrics and analyzing its emotional tone and narrative themes. In my edit, I focused on aligning visual rhythm with musical pacing—using quicker cuts during high-energy moments and slower, more deliberate sequencing during softer sections. Rather than simply illustrating the lyrics, I aimed to interpret both the literal meaning and the underlying story through visual storytelling.

Edited using Adobe Premiere Pro and other Adobe Creative Cloud tools, my version was selected for presentation in my school’s annual exhibition, reflecting both conceptual depth and technical execution.
