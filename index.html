Welcome

Hi! I’m Zahra Batool — a Communication Design student who enjoys creating thoughtful, story-driven visuals through posters, illustration, and layout design. I work mostly with Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, and Premiere Pro, using them to shape designs that feel balanced, expressive, and meaningful. Inspired by atmosphere, landscapes, and storytelling, my goal is to create work that connects visually while reflecting my evolving creative voice.
